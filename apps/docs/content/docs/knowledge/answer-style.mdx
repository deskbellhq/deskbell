The assistant answers from your content under a fixed instruction: use only
the retrieved pages, cite what you used, refuse when nothing fits. That
instruction decides *what* an answer may say. **Settings → Answer style** is
where you decide how it sounds.

## What goes there

A short paragraph, written to the model, in any language:

```text
Answer in two or three short sentences. Address the visitor as "you".
Use plain words and no exclamation marks. Answer in the visitor's language.
```

Tone, length, how the visitor is addressed, which language to answer in, a
house rule about formatting — anything that would be true of every answer.
Up to 2,000 characters. Leave it empty and the assistant keeps the default
voice.

It is per project: every project in a deployment has its own, and a
deployment with one project sets it once.

## What does not go there

Knowledge. The paragraph is added *after* the rules that keep answers grounded
in your content, and it does not become a source: a fact written here is never
cited, never shows on the Conversations page as a citation, and never counts
on the Gaps page. Put facts in your sources, or in a
[written answer](/knowledge/written-answers), where they get cited like any
other page.

Instructions that fight the grounding rules — "always answer, never say you
don't know" — are read by the model like any other, and they cost you exactly
what they sound like they cost. The refusal, the sources line and
[answer verification](/knowledge/answer-verification) still run; a style that
argues with them makes answers worse, not freer.

## When it applies

Saving takes effect on the next answer. A conversation that is already open
follows the new style from its next turn, since the instruction is built per
question. There is no restart and no re-indexing.

## Measuring it

The style is part of the instruction the model reads, so it is part of what
[the evaluation harness](/operate/evaluation) measures: a run against a project
replays the project's style, and the prompt fingerprint in the report changes
when the style does. Before and after is the way to know whether "be brief"
cost you correct answers.
