Once the widget is on your site, the dashboard is where you watch what visitors
ask, what the answers were grounded on, and what your content is missing. The
Overview page is the summary; the pages in this section are the detail.

## Getting started, then the numbers

A fresh project shows a three-step guide on the Overview page instead of
metrics: add your docs, ask it something, put it on your site. The third step
completes when the widget's install beacon arrives from a host that is not the
dashboard's own. After that, or after you dismiss the guide, the page shows
four cards, a chart and the latest conversations.

| Card | Counts | Beside it |
|---|---|---|
| Conversations | Threads in this project | How many need attention — uncertain, marked down, or asking for a person — linking to that view of the inbox; the last seven days' count beneath |
| Questions asked | Visitor messages | The last seven days |
| Answer rate | Certain answers as a share of all answers | Open gaps over the last 30 days |
| Positive feedback | Thumbs-up as a share of all votes | The number of votes |

The chart is 90 days of answers, certain against uncertain, one column per day,
using the rule below. Click a column to open the Conversations page narrowed to
the threads that had a message that day. The list beneath it is the newest
conversations with a preview of the first answer.

## What uncertain means

An answer is **uncertain** when the assistant refused, when it answered
from a best match that scored under the weak floor
(`DESKBELL_GAPS_WEAK_SCORE`, `0.35` by default), or when it named something
your content does not contain
([Answer verification](/knowledge/answer-verification)). One uncertain answer marks
the whole thread, which is the same rule the Gaps page uses to decide what
counts as a gap, so the inbox, the thread badge and the gaps list agree.

Certain is a claim about grounding, not correctness: a confidently wrong answer
reads as certain until a visitor marks it down.

## Where each signal goes

| You want to | Page |
|---|---|
| Read what a visitor asked and what was cited | [Conversations](/operate/conversations) |
| See which questions your content cannot answer, grouped by meaning | [Gaps](/operate/gaps) |
| Count questions, conversations and visitors over time, split by the context your pages send, grouped by topic, intent and signal | [Usage](/operate/usage) |
| Hear about a thumbs-down the moment it happens | [Notifications](/operate/notifications) |
| Prove answer quality with numbers before and after a change | [Evaluation](/operate/evaluation) |
| Answer a gap yourself | [Written answers](/knowledge/written-answers) |
