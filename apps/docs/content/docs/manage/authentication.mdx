The dashboard signs operators in with email and password, and with Google when
configured. It works on a fresh `docker compose up` with no external identity
provider, which keeps Postgres the only dependency. Sessions are cookies
signed with `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`; passwords are hashed with scrypt.

## The first account

A fresh instance has no users, and `/login` sends you to `/register`. The
first account created becomes the organisation's **owner**. Claim it in the
same sitting as exposing the instance: until you do, the owner seat belongs to
whoever reaches `/register` first.

## Sign-up is closed after that

Once an owner exists, `/register` accepts only invited addresses; anything
else is rejected on the server. People join through
[invitations](/manage/members-and-roles#inviting-someone) or, with Google
configured, through an allowed sign-in domain.

## Email and password

Sign-in and sign-up are rate limited in-process, per instance, on top of the
closed sign-up rule.

**Password reset** needs outbound email. With `SMTP_URL` and `EMAIL_FROM` set,
**Forgot password** sends a single-use link that expires after one hour;
requests are rate limited and answered identically whether or not the account
exists, and a completed reset revokes every other session for that account.
Without outbound email the page says the flow is unavailable, and recovery is
an operator action on the database.

## Sign in with Google

Set `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` and `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` and both `/login` and
`/register` gain a Google button. Register
`<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/callback/google` as the redirect URI with Google.

Who gets in follows the same rule as passwords, with one addition:

| Google account | Result |
|---|---|
| The first account on the instance | Becomes the owner |
| An invited address | Joins with the invited role |
| An address at a listed [sign-in domain](/manage/members-and-roles#sign-in-domains) | Joins as a viewer |
| An existing member | Signs in; a password account with the same email is linked to it |
| Anything else | Refused, with the reason shown on the login page |

Google must report the email as verified. This is a login method, not single
sign-on: nothing forces members through Google, and nothing provisions or
removes them from an identity provider. Enforced sign-on through an
identity provider, SAML and SCIM belong to the Enterprise Edition; see
[Licensing](/developer/licensing).

## What the session depends on

- `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` signs the cookie. Changing it signs everyone out.
- `BETTER_AUTH_URL` is the externally visible origin. Invitation and reset
  links are built from it, and the Google redirect URI has to match it.
- The dashboard and the public widget endpoints never share credentials: the
  `/api/v1/*` routes read no cookies, and the dashboard reads no project key.
  [Security](/manage/security) describes the public side.
